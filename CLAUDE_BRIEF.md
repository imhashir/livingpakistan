# PakCiv — End-to-End Build Brief for Claude Code

## Mission
Build **PakCiv**: a Civilization-style interactive hex-grid data atlas of Pakistan, inspired by Andrej Karpathy's [usaciv](https://karpathy.ai/usaciv/) (from [this X post](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2125779541952246039)).

You own this **end to end**: deep research, downloading and parsing real public datasets, designing creative Pakistan-specific capabilities that go beyond a plain clone, building the full data pipeline + interactive web app, verifying everything visually with headless browser screenshots, and committing & pushing your progress continuously to `origin main` (`https://github.com/imhashir/pakciv`).

## Core Design Principles
1. **Real data only — never fabricate numbers.** Every metric, yield, lens, city stat, and wonder must trace back to a real downloaded dataset with source name, vintage, URL, and honest caveats in an in-app Almanac (`sources.json`). Where a metric is modelled (e.g., district-level Gold/GDP via VIIRS nightlights + Kummu gridded GDP reconciled with provincial/national accounts), label it clearly as modelled.
2. **Make it unmistakably Pakistani, not just a reskinned US map.** Lean into what makes Pakistan's geography, economy, and civilization extraordinary:
   - **15–20 km hex grid** covering Pakistan (including AJK & Gilgit-Baltistan, with clean handling of disputed/boundary conventions as standard in open datasets).
   - **Indus Basin & Irrigation ("Food" & Rivers):** Rivers, barrages, dams (Tarbela, Mangla, Diamer-Bhasha, Sukkur, Guddu, Taunsa, etc.), canal command areas, and agricultural crop dominance (wheat, rice, cotton, sugarcane).
   - **City Districts / Specializations:** Real economic clusters from the 2023 Economic Census (PSIC) & known industrial hubs — e.g. Faisalabad (Textiles), Sialkot (Surgical/Sports/Leather), Karachi (Port/Finance/Manufacturing), Lahore (Tech/Culture/Services), Islamabad/Rawalpindi (Capital/Campus), Gujranwala (Light Engineering), Multan (Agri/Cotton), Peshawar (Trade/Frontier), Quetta (Minerals/Trade), Gwadar (Deep-Sea Port).
   - **Energy & Strategic Resources:** Power plants (hydro, nuclear, coal/Thar, RLNG, wind/Jhimpir-Gharo, solar/Quaid-e-Azam Solar Park), mines & minerals (Thar coal, Reko Diq, Saindak, Khewra Salt Mine, marble/chromite).
   - **Wonders (Natural & Cultural):** K2 / Karakoram, Nanga Parbat, Mohenjo-daro, Taxila, Lahore Fort & Shalimar Gardens, Badshahi Mosque, Makli Necropolis, Rohtas Fort, Faisal Mosque, Hingol National Park, Deosai Plains, Karakoram Highway, top universities (NUST, LUMS, GIKI, Aga Khan, etc.).
   - **Pakistan-native Lenses:** Population density, Literacy, Multidimensional Poverty (MPI), Urban share, Mother Tongue / Languages, Religion / Faith, Elevation & Terrain, Land Cover (Farmland / Forest / Desert / Glacier / Built-up), Nightlights / Modelled GDP (Gold), Energy & Grid, 2022 Mega-Floods & Seismic Hazard (USGS Earthquakes).
   - **City View, Advisors & District Explorer:** Click any major city for its City View (yields, districts, workforce/citizens, local wonders, nearby resources); Advisors panel (Land & Water, Energy & Resources, People, Prosperity/MPI, Knowledge, Commerce, Culture); District Explorer (sortable table + interactive scatter plot linked to the hex map across all ~160 districts).
   - **Go further creatively:** Add thoughtful extra capabilities that elevate the experience — e.g., CPEC / motorway & railway trade-route overlay, seasonal flood/monsoon risk toggle, district comparison mode, search/jump-to-district or city, or interactive yield/lens storytelling.

## Creative Pakistan-Specific Expansion (From Hashir — Push Beyond a Clone)
Hashir explicitly asked to **be creative and add exciting, unmistakably Pakistani features** that make PakCiv feel alive, culturally resonant, and addictive to explore (always backed by real datasets/facts, never fabricated numbers):
1. **Civilization Eras / Historical Timeline Scrubber (5,000+ Years of Civilization):** An optional era overlay or toggle highlighting where ancient & historical civilizations flourished on the exact same hexes — Indus Valley (Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Mehrgarh), Gandhara (Taxila, Takht-i-Bahi), Mughal Heartland (Lahore, Multan, Thatta, Rohtas), British Canal Colonies (Lyallpur/Faisalabad, Sargodha, Sukkur Barrage), and Modern Pakistan (Islamabad, Gwadar, CPEC).
2. **The Indus Lifeblood Simulation / Water Cascade:** Show the Indus + 5 Punjab rivers (Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Sutlej, Beas) + Kabul River flowing from the Karakoram/Himalayan glaciers down through major dams & barrages (Tarbela, Mangla, Chashma, Taunsa, Guddu, Sukkur, Kotri) into the world's largest contiguous irrigation network — with an interactive highlight of the Indus Water Treaty (Western vs Eastern rivers) and how irrigation drives Food yields across the Punjab & Sindh plains vs arid Balochistan/Thar.
3. **Glacier-to-Sea Elevation & Climate Journey ("0m to 8,611m"):** Highlight Pakistan's extraordinary vertical diversity — Arabian Sea mangroves (Indus Delta), Thar & Cholistan deserts, fertile Indus alluvium, Potohar Plateau, Suleiman/Kirthar ranges, and the junction of the Himalayas, Karakoram, and Hindu Kush (5 of the world's 14 eight-thousanders: K2, Nanga Parbat, Gasherbrum I, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum II, plus Siachen/Baltoro/Biafo glaciers).
4. **2022 Mega-Flood & Climate Vulnerability Lens:** Overlay the real 2022 Indus Basin flood inundation footprint, glacier-lake outburst flood (GLOF) zones in GB/KP, heatwave corridors (Jacobabad/Sibi/Turbat), and USGS seismic fault lines (2005 Kashmir, 2013 Awaran, 1935 Quetta) so users can see the human and economic stakes.
5. **Trade Routes & Corridors ("Caravans & Corridors"):** Toggleable overlay for the **Grand Trunk Road (GT Road)**, **Karakoram Highway (KKH / Silk Road)**, **CPEC Western & Eastern alignments** (Gwadar ↔ Khunjerab Pass), **ML-1 Main Line Railway** (Karachi–Lahore–Peshawar), **Motorways (M-1 to M-5)**, and **Karachi / Port Qasim / Gwadar** maritime gateways.
6. **Cultural Mosaic & Linguistic Tapestry:** Interactive Mother Tongue / Language lens (Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi, Saraiki, Urdu, Balochi, Hindko, Brahui, Kashmiri, Shina/Burushaski/Balti/Khowar/Wakhi in GB/Chitral) + Craft, Cuisine & Heritage pins (e.g., Multan blue pottery, Ajrak/Sindhi topi, Sialkot footballs & surgical instruments, Chiniot woodwork, Peshawar Qissa Khwani, Truck Art hubs, Sufi shrines like Data Darbar, Lal Shahbaz Qalandar, Shah Abdul Latif Bhittai, Shah Rukn-e-Alam).
7. **Cricket & Sports Heritage / University & Tech Hubs:** Optional pins/badges in City View or a Culture/Knowledge sub-layer for iconic cricket stadiums (Gaddafi, National Stadium, Pindi, Multan, Niaz, Bugti) and major tech/university clusters (Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi).
8. **Interactive "Explore & Discover" Mode:**
   - **Guided Story Tours ("Chronicles of Pakistan"):** 5–6 one-click camera fly-to stories (e.g. *"From K2 to the Arabian Sea"*, *"The Indus Food Machine"*, *"Made in Pakistan: The Industrial Belt"*, *"5,000 Years of Wonders"*, *"The 2022 Deluge"*), panning/zooming the hex map and switching lenses automatically with concise real-data captions.
   - **District Duel / Side-by-Side Comparison:** Pick any two districts (e.g. Lahore vs Karachi, Faisalabad vs Sialkot, Skardu vs Gwadar) and compare their Civ yields, census stats, literacy, MPI, terrain, and specializations head-to-head.
   - **Random "Hidden Gem" / "Fly to Wonder" button** + instant fuzzy search across all ~160 districts, cities, wonders, dams, and mountain peaks.

## Hard Frontend Architecture Requirement (From Hashir)
- **100% browser-runnable static web app** (zero runtime backend required — all processed datasets ship as static JSON/binary assets served alongside the bundle so it deploys cleanly to static hosting like Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages).
- **Entirely Three.js-based renderer** (WebGL via `three` — true 3D/2.5D hex-grid map with elevation relief from 0m Arabian Sea to 8,611m K2, extruded/layered hex tiles, rivers, roads/corridors, 3D city/wonder markers, smooth orbit/pan/zoom camera, raycasting hover/click, and crisp HUD overlays). Do NOT build a flat 2D Canvas renderer; use Three.js throughout.
